Reflection
When machines take over tasks, people need more leadership, not less: meaning, fairness and judgement cannot be automated.Core message · Unit 8 · ILO 4, 1
1
When the complication hit your team’s pitch today, what did you personally decide or say — and what would you do differently in the real Panel Defense?
2
Look at your team’s decision log: where did AI genuinely improve your recommendation, and where would relying on it have weakened your ability to defend the decision yourself?
3
Think of a task in your own field that AI will take over within three years: what would you need — in autonomy, competence and relatedness — to stay motivated, and how would you lead a team through that transition?

Guidance

Write 300–400 words within 48 hours. Your answer to the third question can serve as the closing section of your Assessment 2 dossier.
