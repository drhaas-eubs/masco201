Reflection
The style you believe you lead with matters less than the style your people experience when you are under pressure.Core message · Unit 3 · ILO 1, 2
1
In the Case Study Duel you held or conceded a style classification. What evidence made you decide, and would you decide the same way if the leader in the episode had been you?
2
Where did the AI's style classification help you see something, and where did you overrule it? What does that tell you about using AI to judge people rather than texts?
3
Think of the last time someone brought you bad news. Using Edmondson's three moves, what exactly did you do — and what will you do differently next time?

Guidance

Answer within 48 hours, about 150 words per question. Your answers to questions 1 and 3 can feed directly into the self-assessment section of your Assessment 2 dossier.
