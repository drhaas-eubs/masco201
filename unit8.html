Core message · Unit 8
When machines take over tasks, people need more leadership, not less: meaning, fairness and judgement cannot be automated.
  • AI now drafts, analyses and decides parts of work that used to define people’s jobs and professional identity.
  • The tension is that efficiency gains arrive faster than people can let go of the old role and find value in the new one.
  • This unit trains you to lead that transition — keeping people motivated, keeping humans accountable and defending your judgement under pressure.

Driving question

When the algorithm and your judgement disagree, who do you answer to?

Unit content (course outline)

  • Keeping teams motivated when automation replaces tasks
  • Making ethical calls when the algorithm says otherwise
  • Which leadership skills. empathy, judgment, moral courage, AI cannot replicate
  • Leading through the uncertainty of continuous technological change

AI integration in this unit

AI is both subject and tool in the capstone: teams use it to research their case, map which tasks fall inside or outside the jagged frontier and generate counter-arguments to their recommendation. The recommendation, the ethical call, the people plan and the live defence stay human — and the panel will test exactly that. AI drafts, the leader decides, and every AI contribution is declared in the team’s decision log.
