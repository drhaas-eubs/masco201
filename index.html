This is the working environment for MASCO201. The module is a self-exploration journey into what it means to lead people, not processes. Every unit has its own framework gallery in two Slibraries: established leadership frameworks and an AI Slibrary that shows what AI changes at exactly this point of leadership practice — and what stays distinctly human.

Module profile

Unifying threadLeading in the Age of AI: leading people, not processes
Unit of analysisThe individual leader — identity, judgement and influence
RegisterLeadership development: emotional intelligence, adaptive leadership, crisis and stakeholders
AI angleAI informs, the leader decides — empathy, judgement and moral courage stay human
Builds onMASCO101 Tools for Success (Self) · leads to MASCO301 Building Successful Teams (Team)
