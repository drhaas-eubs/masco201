Course description

This module is anchored around a single unifying thread: Leading in the Age of AI: a self-exploration journey into what it means to lead people, not processes. Every unit invites students to build a clearer, more honest picture of themselves as leaders and to test that identity against real dilemmas, real pressure, and real stakeholders. As more decisions become automated and data-driven, the leadership capabilities that matter most, empathy, judgment, moral courage, and the ability to earn trust without formal authority, remain distinctly human. This course equips students with the self-awareness, frameworks, and practical tools needed to lead through uncertainty, ethical tension, and organizational change, in person and across hybrid and virtual environments.

Intended learning outcomes

  1. Critically discuss the approaches to contemporary strategic leadership in organizations.
  2. Develop a comprehensive leadership development plan to enhance their own career prospects.
  3. Critically evaluate how strategic leaders can achieve stakeholder collaboration to support organizational success.
  4. Demonstrate the significance of leading strategically during periods of change and turbulence.
