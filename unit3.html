Core message · Unit 3
The style you believe you lead with matters less than the style your people experience when you are under pressure.
  • Most leaders rely on one or two default styles and believe they are far more flexible than they are.
  • Under pressure those defaults narrow further, and the people around you quietly stop telling you what you need to hear.
  • This unit trains you to diagnose your own emotional intelligence and style range, and to see the speaking-up climate you personally create.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
1Critically discuss the approaches to contemporary strategic leadership in organizations.
2Develop a comprehensive leadership development plan to enhance their own career prospects.
Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.

Driving question

When did someone last decide not to tell you something — and what in your behaviour made silence the safer choice?

Unit content (course outline)

  • “Leading with Empathy: The Power of Emotionally Intelligent Leadership” - Wrap-up of Boostcamp Episode
  • Emotional Intelligence as a leadership competency: Goleman’s five components (self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skill)
  • Goleman’s Six Leadership Styles: what each style looks like in practice and when to use it
  • Classroom exercise: identifying leadership styles in real case examples
  • Psychological safety as a leader's individual responsibility (Edmondson): the specific behaviors that create or destroy it, and why teams underperform when leaders unintentionally suppress dissent

AI integration in this unit

AI can transcribe a meeting, count who spoke and suggest which style a leader used, but it cannot feel the room or carry a relationship. In this unit students use an AI tool only on anonymised case texts to propose a style classification, then argue against it from the evidence. Emotion-recognition tools aimed at colleagues are off-limits: the EU AI Act prohibits them in the workplace. AI drafts, the leader decides, and every AI contribution is declared.
