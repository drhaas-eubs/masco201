Role Play — Hybrid Stakeholder Summit
Core message · Unit 6
In hybrid work, stakeholders judge your leadership by the channel you choose before they hear what you say.
  • Most managers now lead stakeholders they rarely meet in person, across sites, time zones and cultures.
  • The tension is that the stakeholders with the most power or urgency are often the least visible, and lean digital channels strip out the cues that build trust.
  • This unit trains leaders to map stakeholder salience and to design engagement — channel, rhythm and technology — that keeps collaboration alive at a distance.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
3Critically evaluate how strategic leaders can achieve stakeholder collaboration to support organizational success.
Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.
Session question
Can you win the support of stakeholders you cannot all see in the same room?
1
Boostcamp wrap-up: building bridges in virtual teams
15 min

Pairs name one intercultural misunderstanding from the episode and place it on the Culture Map scales from MASCO101. The lecturer links it to affinity distance: culture becomes harder to read through a screen.

Output: Three examples linked to virtual distance

2
Identify and map the stakeholders
30 min

Teams read the Hafenwerk Logistics case and build a salience map. The power-interest grid from MASCO101 is used only as a quick first sort.

  1. List every group that affects or is affected by the rollout
  2. Score power, legitimacy and urgency for each
  3. Identify definitive and dangerous stakeholders
  4. Mark which stakeholders are remote, on site or hybrid

Output: Salience map on A3

3
Design the engagement
20 min

For the three most salient stakeholders, teams choose channel, rhythm and sequence using media richness and media synchronicity: what is conveyed asynchronously and where convergence happens live.

Output: Engagement plan for three stakeholders

Break · 10 minutes
4
Hybrid stakeholder summit
45 min

Half of the stakeholder roles join from the room, half online through a video call. Each team's operations lead runs a 12-minute summit to win support for a revised rollout plan; role cards contain hidden concerns and one urgency trigger. Observers track who speaks, who is ignored and which cues are lost online.

Output: Observer log per summit

5
AI listening test
15 min

Teams paste the anonymised summit notes into an approved AI tool, ask it to cluster stakeholder concerns, and compare the result with their own salience map. Which concern did the AI miss or misweight, and could its output be explained to the works council?

Output: Comparison note and AI-use log

6
Debrief and declaration
15 min

Plenary on what changed between in-room and online stakeholders. Each student writes one rule for their own hybrid leadership practice and declares how AI was used.

Output: Personal hybrid-leadership rule

Deliverable: A stakeholder salience map with an engagement plan for the three most salient stakeholders, observer notes from the summit and an AI-use declaration — directly usable for Assessment 3.

Materials

  • Hafenwerk Logistics case (printed)
  • Salience mapping template (A3)
  • Stakeholder role cards with hidden concerns
  • Video-call link and second screen
  • Observer logs
  • Laptops with an approved AI tool
  • Timer

On-screen study only — copying, downloading and printing are disabled.
