Boardroom Role Play — Challenge Upward
Core message · Unit 5
Knowing what is right is common; saying it to someone with power over you is the leadership skill.
  • Most ethical failures in organisations are not failures of knowledge: people usually sense that something is wrong.
  • The tension arises when doing the right thing costs something — a deadline, a bonus, a relationship with a superior — and when the dilemma is right against right rather than right against wrong.
  • This unit trains moral courage as a practised skill: diagnosing the dilemma, scripting the voice and rehearsing the challenge upward.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
1Critically discuss the approaches to contemporary strategic leadership in organizations.
4Demonstrate the significance of leading strategically during periods of change and turbulence.
Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.
Session question
Can you raise a concern to a senior leader so that it is heard, not dismissed?
1
Boostcamp wrap-up: doing things right vs. doing the right thing
15 min

Pairs share one moment from the episode where compliance and ethics pulled apart. The lecturer links it to Rest's four components: at which gate did the people in the episode stop?

Output: Three shared examples on the board

2
Diagnose the dilemma
25 min

Teams read the Vireon Diagnostics case and classify the dilemma with Kidder's right-vs-right paradigms.

  1. Separate right-vs-wrong elements from right-vs-right ones
  2. Name the paradigm and the two values in conflict
  3. Test the options with ends-based, rule-based and care-based reasoning
  4. Look for a third option

Output: One-page dilemma diagnosis

3
Courage map and real-case vignette
20 min

Short input on the Volkswagen emissions case (2015) as a documented example of organisational silence. Each student maps Kidder's three elements — principles, danger, endurance — for the Vireon product lead and for themselves.

Output: Personal courage map

Break · 10 minutes
4
Script lab with an AI sparring partner
25 min

Each student writes a two-minute script in the Giving Voice to Values format. A generative AI tool then plays the sceptical COO and raises the three strongest rationalisations; the student rewrites the script. The AI never drafts the script.

  1. Write opening, concern, evidence and proposal
  2. Ask the AI for the three strongest counter-arguments
  3. Revise the script and log the AI use

Output: Revised script and AI-use log

5
Boardroom role play
40 min

Trios rotate the roles of speaker, senior leader and observer. The senior leader receives a private card with pressures (board meeting, contract, bonus). Each round lasts 8 minutes plus 4 minutes of observer feedback on clarity, evidence, tone and proposal.

Output: Three observer feedback sheets per trio

6
Debrief and declaration
15 min

Plenary on what made a challenge land. Students note one phrase they will reuse and declare how AI was used in the session.

Output: Personal phrase bank entry

Deliverable: A rehearsed two-minute 'challenge upward' script with observer feedback and an AI-use declaration — a building block for the Assessment 2 development plan.

Materials

  • Vireon Diagnostics case (printed)
  • Kidder paradigm worksheet
  • Giving Voice to Values script template
  • Role cards for the senior leader
  • Observer feedback sheets
  • Laptops with an approved AI tool
  • Timer

On-screen study only — copying, downloading and printing are disabled.
