Hackathon — Human Edge Hackathon
Core message · Unit 8
When machines take over tasks, people need more leadership, not less: meaning, fairness and judgement cannot be automated.
  • AI now drafts, analyses and decides parts of work that used to define people’s jobs and professional identity.
  • The tension is that efficiency gains arrive faster than people can let go of the old role and find value in the new one.
  • This unit trains you to lead that transition — keeping people motivated, keeping humans accountable and defending your judgement under pressure.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
4Demonstrate the significance of leading strategically during periods of change and turbulence.
1Critically discuss the approaches to contemporary strategic leadership in organizations.
Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.
Session question
Can your team defend a human call on an AI decision when the facts change mid-pitch?
1
Hackathon brief
10 min

Teams of 3–4 (the Assessment 3 teams) receive the capstone case. Rules: at least two frameworks from different units, one decision log, every AI contribution declared.

Output: Team roles and decision log opened

2
Sprint 1: diagnose
35 min

AI may be used for research and counter-arguments; the diagnosis is the team’s.

  1. Map the case tasks inside and outside the jagged frontier
  2. Diagnose the people side with Self-Determination Theory and Bridges
  3. Check Meaningful Human Control: who tracks, who traces?
  4. Choose two further frameworks from Units 1–7 (e.g. Heifetz, Kidder, Salience)

Output: One-page diagnosis

3
Sprint 2: recommend
30 min

Teams write a recommendation and a people plan on one A3 sheet: the decision, who is accountable, what changes for the people affected, the first 90 days, and the core message in one sentence.

Output: A3 recommendation canvas

Break · 10 minutes
4
Panel rehearsal with complication
50 min

Each team presents for 5 minutes to a panel of lecturer and two peers. At minute 3 the panel introduces an unannounced complication. The team has 2 minutes to adapt, followed by individual questions to named members.

  1. 5 min pitch
  2. Unannounced complication
  3. 2 min adaptation
  4. 3 min individual Q&A

Output: Panel feedback against the Assessment 3 criteria

5
Debrief and course synthesis
15 min

Plenary: which human capabilities did the complication reveal — empathy, judgement, moral courage? Each student writes the one leadership habit from the course they will keep.

Output: Personal synthesis note for the Assessment 2 dossier

Deliverable: An A3 recommendation canvas with decision log (AI use declared) and written panel feedback that the team uses to prepare the Assessment 3 Panel Defense.

Materials

  • Capstone case and complication cards
  • A3 recommendation canvas
  • Assessment 3 criteria sheet for the panel
  • Laptops with an approved AI tool
  • Timer and buzzer

On-screen study only — copying, downloading and printing are disabled.
