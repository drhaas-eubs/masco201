Diagnostic Studio — True North Lab
Core message · Unit 2
You cannot lead from a story you have never told yourself honestly — and others already see the version you avoid.
  • Authentic leaders lead from their own life story and from values they have tested under pressure, not from a borrowed style.
  • The difficulty is that most people overestimate how clearly they know their values and underestimate the gap between their intentions and how others experience them.
  • This unit trains students to name their values, read their crucibles and plan the 360-degree feedback that will close that gap.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
2Develop a comprehensive leadership development plan to enhance their own career prospects.
Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.
Session question
What would the people who work with you say your values are — and would they be right?
1
Boostcamp wrap-up: from manager to leader
15 min

Pairs share one moment from the Boostcamp episode “From Manager to Leader” that matched or contradicted their own experience. Link the discussion to Kotter's manager–leader distinction from Unit 1.

2
Values sort
25 min

Students rank Schwartz's ten values, then identify the two values they would defend under pressure and the two they would trade first.

  1. Rank the ten values individually
  2. Mark opposing pairs on the circle
  3. Write one real situation where two values collided

Output: Personal values map

3
Lifeline and crucible
30 min

Students draw their lifeline with high and low points and select one crucible. Using Gibbs' cycle, an AI assistant may ask probing questions, but only after the student has applied the Disclosure Boundary.

  1. Draw the lifeline on A3
  2. Select one crucible experience
  3. Classify what you will and will not share with AI
  4. Answer AI questions stage by stage and log the prompts

Output: First draft of the crucible narrative

Break · 15 minutes
4
True North compass
25 min

Using George's five dimensions, students write one sentence per dimension and share it with a trio. Peers ask the question George's interviews kept asking: when did you act on this value when it cost you something?

Output: True North compass, version 1

5
360 design clinic
30 min

Students design the 360-degree feedback exercise for Assessment 2: whom to ask, which questions, how responses stay anonymous, and how AI-assisted synthesis will be checked.

  1. Choose 5–8 respondents across roles
  2. Draft 6 questions linked to the Leadership Circle Profile
  3. Plan anonymisation and the sycophancy check

Output: 360 feedback plan

6
Declaration and next step
10 min

Each student writes the gap they expect the 360 to reveal and declares how AI was used today. Brief the Boostcamp episode “Leading with Empathy” before Unit 3.

Deliverable: Dossier building blocks for Assessment 2: values map, crucible draft, True North compass v1 and a 360 feedback plan, with an AI-use declaration.

Materials

  • Schwartz values cards
  • A3 lifeline sheets
  • True North compass template
  • 360 feedback plan template
  • Laptops with an approved AI tool
  • Timer

On-screen study only — copying, downloading and printing are disabled.
