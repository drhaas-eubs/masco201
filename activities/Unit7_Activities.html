Pitch Arena — Thumbnail Presence Pitch
Core message · Unit 7
Authority is lent, not owned: people follow you only for as long as your character, competence and consistency keep earning it.
  • Most early-career leaders must move people who do not report to them, so a title rarely does the work.
  • The tension is that people judge you on your impact over time, while you judge yourself on your intentions in the moment.
  • This unit trains you to build personal power and credibility deliberately — and to make your message land with ethos, pathos and logos.
Intended learning outcomes addressed
2Develop a comprehensive leadership development plan to enhance their own career prospects.
3Critically evaluate how strategic leaders can achieve stakeholder collaboration to support organizational success.
Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.
Session question
Can you earn a room’s trust in 90 seconds without your title?
1
Hook: the title test
15 min

Two short scenes: the same request made by a new graduate and by a director. Students list what the graduate would need to get the same yes. Map the answers onto French & Raven’s positional and personal bases.

Output: Class map of power bases

2
Power audit and credibility diagnosis
25 min
  1. Score yourself 1–5 on each of the six power bases
  2. Score yourself on Covey's four cores
  3. Swap with a partner who has worked with you this term and compare scores
  4. Name the one gap between your intent and your impact

Output: Personal power and credibility audit (feeds the Assessment 2 dossier)

3
Thumbnail sketching
25 min

In the JD Meier thumbnail tradition, each student draws three quick sketches of their credibility message on one A4 sheet: who I am as a leader, the evidence, the ask. Then distil the three sketches into one core sentence after Bostelaar: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.

Output: Thumbnail sheet with a one-sentence core message

Break · 10 minutes
4
AI rehearsal
20 min

AI stays in rehearsal: no generated scripts are read aloud.

  1. Paste your core message and ask the AI tool to act as a sceptical stakeholder
  2. Collect the three hardest questions
  3. Check your logos: does the argument hold?
  4. Note on the pitch card what the AI contributed

Output: Three hard questions and a revised message

5
Pitch Arena
40 min

Groups of six. Each student delivers a 90-second pitch without slides to a stakeholder they do not manage (played by peers). Listeners score ethos, pathos and logos 1–5 and write one sentence on the impact they actually experienced.

Output: Peer scores and impact notes per student

6
Debrief: intent vs impact
15 min

Students compare the impact notes with their intended message. Plenary: which corner of the triangle was weakest across the class, and which power base did the strongest pitches draw on? Link to executive presence (Hewlett 2014; MASCO101 Unit 5).

Output: One commitment for the 12-month development plan

Deliverable: A thumbnail sheet with a one-sentence core message, the peer scores and one development commitment for the Assessment 2 dossier, with the AI use declared.

Materials

  • A4 thumbnail sheets and markers
  • Power-base and Four Cores audit sheet
  • Ethos-Pathos-Logos scoring cards
  • Laptops with an approved AI tool
  • Timer

On-screen study only — copying, downloading and printing are disabled.
