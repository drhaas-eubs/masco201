Core message · Unit 1
Classical leadership theories are hypotheses about people, not answers; effective leaders test them against the situation in front of them.
  • Leadership theory has moved from traits to behaviour, contingency, transformation and adaptation, and each school captured something real.
  • Yet no single theory survives contact with a volatile, AI-shaped workplace in which tasks are automated and the problems that remain are human ones.
  • This unit trains students to reduce each theory to its first premises, test it against evidence and choose deliberately what to use.

Driving question

If AI can do much of what managers do, what is left that only a leader can do?

Unit content (course outline)

  • What does effective leadership actually look like today?
  • Why classical leadership theories are a starting point, not an answer: a critical review
  • Introduction to Heifetz’s distinction between technical and adaptive problems
  • Characteristics of effective contemporary leaders
  • Challenges in contemporary leadership
  • Boostcamp module to be completed before starting Unit 2: Episode “From Manager to Leader: Embracing the Art of Leadership”

AI integration in this unit

AI enters this unit as the context leaders now work in, not as a tool to admire. Students use an AI assistant to generate the strongest case for and against each leadership theory before the Theory Tribunal, then test those arguments against Yukl and the evidence. The judgement on which theory holds — and for which situation — stays human. Standing course rule: AI drafts, the student decides, and every AI contribution is declared.
