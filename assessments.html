Assessment 2 — Leadership Identity & Development Dossier

40% · Individual dossier

An individual dossier assigned as part of independent learning and outside of class hours. Students combine a self- and 360-degree leadership diagnostic with an analysis of a real leadership experience, culminating in a verifiable 12-month development plan. Components: Goleman's Six Leadership Styles self-assessment; structured 360-degree feedback exercise (input from peers, manager, or direct reports); leadership "crucible" analysis of a real experience, using Heifetz's technical vs. adaptive framework; 12-month Leadership Development Plan with concrete, verifiable actions and checkpoints tied to the gaps identified.

Assessment 3 — Leadership Case Analysis: Live Crisis Simulation & Panel Defense

40% · Group (3–4) with individual accountability

A group assessment assigned as part of independent learning and outside of class hours. Teams analyze a real leadership case and defend their recommendation live to a panel, which introduces an unannounced complication and questions individual members directly, with peer evaluation informing the individual grade. Teams apply at least two module frameworks to a real leadership case.
