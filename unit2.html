Core message · Unit 2
You cannot lead from a story you have never told yourself honestly — and others already see the version you avoid.
  • Authentic leaders lead from their own life story and from values they have tested under pressure, not from a borrowed style.
  • The difficulty is that most people overestimate how clearly they know their values and underestimate the gap between their intentions and how others experience them.
  • This unit trains students to name their values, read their crucibles and plan the 360-degree feedback that will close that gap.

Core message distilled after Bostelaar (2024), Clarify Your Core Message: decompose, prioritise, three sentences, one sentence.

Driving question

Which of your values would you keep if keeping it cost you the next promotion?

Unit content (course outline)

  • “From Manager to Leader: Embracing the Art of Leadership” - Wrap-up of Boostcamp Episode
  • Bill George’s Authentic Leadership model: leading from your own story
  • How personal values drive leadership behavior under pressure
  • The gap between who you think you are as a leader and how others actually experience you
  • Structured self-reflection exercise
  • Boostcamp module to be completed before starting Unit 3: Episode “Leading with Empathy: The Power of Emotionally Intelligent Leadership”

AI integration in this unit

AI is used here as a reflection partner, not as a judge of character. Students may use an AI assistant to ask probing questions about their life story and to structure their reflection with Gibbs' cycle, but they first decide what they will never share and run a sycophancy check on any flattering feedback. The meaning of their story and the choice of their values stay human. AI drafts, the student decides, and every AI contribution is declared.
