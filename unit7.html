Core message · Unit 7
Authority is lent, not owned: people follow you only for as long as your character, competence and consistency keep earning it.
  • Most early-career leaders must move people who do not report to them, so a title rarely does the work.
  • The tension is that people judge you on your impact over time, while you judge yourself on your intentions in the moment.
  • This unit trains you to build personal power and credibility deliberately — and to make your message land with ethos, pathos and logos.

Driving question

Why would anyone follow you if you had no title at all?

Unit content (course outline)

  • French and Raven’s power bases and the concept of executive presence
  • How you are perceived as a leader over time: consistency and credibility
  • The gap between your intentions and your actual impact on people
  • Building trust and credibility without formal authority

AI integration in this unit

AI is a rehearsal partner in this unit: students use it to stress-test their credibility pitch, generate sceptical audience questions and check the logic of their argument. Presence, eye contact, the story and the judgement of what to say stay human, and no AI-generated voice, video or avatar may stand in for the student. AI drafts, the student decides, and every AI contribution is declared on the pitch card.
